# Crashout Recovery — Simple Ops Guide

**Pause before posting. Build steadier habits.**

Crashout Recovery gives you a calmer place to turn a hot thought into a safer next move.

## 🚀 Start Here

1. Tap **+ Draft**.
2. Write what is on your mind.
3. Tap **Read tone & suggest a move** or pause briefly for an automatic vibe check.
4. Choose one safe move.
5. Save your draft idea.

**🔥 Nice safe move!** Small pauses build stronger habits.

## 🍳 Everyday Recipes

### 🔥 About to post something risky

**Draft → Read vibe → Safe move → Save draft idea**

Keep the first version private. You can always post later.

### 🌶️ The feed feels spicy

**Tap a flashing chip → Read Signals → Save a draft idea**

🌍 Watch the world mood before matching its energy.

### ✨ Want better wording

**Draft → Compare calm, clear, and stable options → Edit your choice**

Reviewed staff examples appear first. AI-assisted wording is clearly labeled when it fills a gap.

### 📈 Want to check your habits

**Creator → Progress meter + win streak**

Look for consistency, not perfection.

## 🔥 Streak & Momentum

Your **🔥 win streak** grows when you save draft ideas or take safe moves on different days.

The progress meter keeps it simple:

- 🌱 **Low** — start with one small safe move
- 🚶 **Medium** — momentum is building
- 🚀 **High** — your steadier habits are holding

Every saved draft and safe choice can move you forward.

## 🎬 Moments

- Tap a clip to watch it in the YouTube player.
- If a clip is not ready, use its YouTube search option.
- Creator and Pro members can tap **Add Video**.
- Enter a search phrase and optionally paste a YouTube video ID or link.
- Added videos join the shared Moments collection for everyone.

🎥 Feature clips that help people pause, reflect, or recover.

## 🧠 Smart Rewrites

Rewrite choices appear directly in the Draft box:

- 🌿 **Calm version** — lowers the heat
- 🔎 **Clear rewrite** — focuses the main point
- ⚖️ **Stable phrasing** — keeps the next move reversible
- 🧭 **Recovery actions** — suggest a small, safer next step

Staff-reviewed content is the first choice. **AI-assisted** options only fill uncovered gaps.

## ♻️ Reset Your Demo

Open **Help / Ops** and choose **Reset browser cache** to clear the drafts, streaks, and signals stored in that browser.

For a clean guest reset, log out first. Resetting the browser does not delete a signed-in account, and saved account information may return when you sign in again.

## 💛 Remember

You do not have to solve everything at once. Pause, make one reversible choice, and keep moving.

**✨ One small move beats one big crashout.**
