Crashout Recovery

This moment feels intense. Don't make an irreversible move.

Next step: Draft, don't delete — post a tiny test instead of the full thing.

Do this instead

Micro-action: Stop. Pick one reversible move.

Next step: Write the next move, not the final action.

  • Stop. Step away before you act.
  • Write privately. Get the reaction out of your system first.
  • Take one action. A small, controlled step beats a blow-up.
  • Change one thing. Adjust one variable, not everything.
  • Rest. Reset your head before you decide anything big.

You're in charge. Make the move you won't regret tomorrow.
